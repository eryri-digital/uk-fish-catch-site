UK Fish Catch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

UK Fish Catch is built by Eryri Digital. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and how it's handled.

Data stored on your device

Your profile, logged trips, logged catches, and any photos you add are stored locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to or stored on any server operated by Eryri Digital.

Location

UK Fish Catch uses your location only while the app is open, to help find nearby UK tide stations and support trip planning. Location is not accessed in the background, and is not used for any other purpose.

Camera and photo library

Camera and photo library access is requested only when you actively choose to take or attach a photo — for example, adding a photo to a catch or a trip. Access is never requested for any unrelated reason.

Tide data

To show tide times and charts, the app sends requests to the UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO) ADMIRALTY tidal data service for the station you select. This is used solely to display tide information within the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

UK Fish Catch Pro is handled entirely through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Eryri Digital does not process, see, or store your payment details — this is managed by Apple under your own Apple ID account.

Advertising

Free-tier users may be shown ads, including a rewarded ad option to unlock specific features, provided through Google AdMob. Consent for personalised advertising is requested in line with UK and EU requirements before any ad is shown. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Analytics

UK Fish Catch does not currently use a separate third-party analytics service. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Your controls

You can review or change location, camera, and photo library permissions for UK Fish Catch at any time in your device's Settings app.

Deleting your data

Deleting UK Fish Catch from your device removes its locally stored data, including your profile, trips, catches, and photos, subject to your device's normal backup behaviour (for example, if included in an iCloud or iTunes backup made before deletion).

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled can be sent to eryridigital.support@gmail.com.
