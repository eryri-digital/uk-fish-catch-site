Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open UK Fish Catch, go to the Pro upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." This uses Apple's own App Store account system, so it works automatically as long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to subscribe.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is only used while the app is open, to find UK tide stations near you and help you plan a fishing trip. It isn't used for anything else, and isn't accessed in the background.

Why does the app ask for camera or photo access?

Camera and photo library access is only requested when you choose to take or attach a photo yourself — for example, adding a photo to a logged catch or trip. It's never requested automatically.

Where does the tide information come from?

Tide times and heights come from the UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO) ADMIRALTY tidal data service. This data is a prediction, not a live observation, and should be used as guidance alongside your own judgement.

Are the fishing rules always up to date?

UK Fish Catch is a guide based on published official sources, but fishing rules can vary by river, district or area, and can change over time. Please check current official sources where it matters before keeping a fish.
